FAQs

What is Pardos Sakura and where is it located?

Pardos Sakura is an upcoming premium 3 & 4 BHK residential project located in Sector 90, New Gurgaon, Gurugram.

What configurations are available at Pardos Sakura?

The project offers 3 and 4 BHK residences, giving families options based on their space requirements, lifestyle preferences and budget.

What amenities are available at Pardos Sakura?

Project highlights include a premium clubhouse, fitness and wellness facilities, landscaped green spaces, and recreational and community spaces.

How is Pardos Sakura connected to major areas?

The location provides access towards Dwarka Expressway, NH-48 and IMT Manesar. Actual travel time can depend on traffic, route and destination, so buyers should check the commute for their regular travel requirements.

What should I verify before buying Pardos Sakura?

Buyers should verify the latest RERA details, approvals, project status, specifications, pricing, payment terms, applicable charges and availability. A site visit and comparison with other Sector 90 projects can also help with the decision.
