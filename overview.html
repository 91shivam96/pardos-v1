Pardos Sakura Gurgaon

Pardos Sakura Gurgaon is an upcoming premium residential project in Sector 90, New Gurgaon, offering 3 and 4 BHK residences for families looking for more space and comfortable living. The project brings together modern architecture, spacious layouts and a range of lifestyle amenities in a well-connected part of Gurugram.


The residences are planned with a focus on practical family living, while landscaped green spaces add an open and pleasant feel to the development. Residents can also expect access to a premium clubhouse along with fitness, wellness, leisure and community spaces.


Location is another key part of the project. Pardos Sakura offers convenient access towards Dwarka Expressway, NH-48 and IMT Manesar, with schools, hospitals, shopping and other daily conveniences available across the surrounding New Gurgaon area.

Prime Location

Pardos Sector 90 is located in New Gurgaon, with convenient access to major roads, business hubs, metro stations and important destinations across Gurugram and Delhi.

The location works well for buyers who want their home within reasonable reach of work, travel and everyday conveniences — Dwarka Expressway, NH-48 and IMT Manesar are all within easy reach.
